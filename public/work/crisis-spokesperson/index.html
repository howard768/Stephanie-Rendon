Crisis communications / Media training

A prepared voice when the stakes are high.

Helping leaders and field spokespeople communicate during national disaster responses.

Organization / PeriodAmerican Red Cross · 2017–2021
My roleMedia relations, executive preparation and bilingual spokesperson

The challenge

Disaster communications brings together immediate public needs, fast-moving information and intense media attention. Leaders and field teams need to speak clearly while staying aligned with the organization’s response.

My contribution

At national headquarters, I prepared the CEO and other senior executives for interviews through talking points, messaging frameworks and one-on-one coaching. During disaster responses, I coordinated satellite media tours, handled incoming reporter calls, and trained and deployed field spokespeople. I also served as an on-air spokesperson in English and Spanish.

Evidence of the work

Across my Red Cross tenure, I trained and deployed more than 100 field spokespeople and helped secure more than 100 national media placements. These are career-period totals, not results attributed to a single response. In May 2020, The New York Times quoted me on Red Cross shelter planning during a hurricane season shaped by COVID-19.

What this work brings to a team

Experience preparing senior leaders, coordinating spokespeople and communicating under pressure, with the ability to speak directly to English- and Spanish-speaking audiences.

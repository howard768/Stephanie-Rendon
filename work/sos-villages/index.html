Integrated campaign / Media relations

Stories that connect children, families and the public.

Building a communications function and bringing a global organization’s work to U.S. audiences.

Organization / PeriodSOS Children’s Villages–USA · 2014–2017
My roleCommunications team, progressing to Director of Communications

The work

At SOS Children’s Villages–USA, I built the communications function and helped bring stories about children and families to national audiences. The work included an integrated campaign with HuffPost and Johnson & Johnson.

My contribution

I developed media pitches and coached executives, board members and program participants for interviews and panels. I pitched NBC News stories about a Syrian boy and a Liberian war survivor, and supported stakeholder communications in English and Spanish across Latin America and the Caribbean.

Research / Higher education

Bringing research into everyday life.

Making public health research understandable and preparing the people behind it to speak with confidence.

Organization / PeriodFlorida International University · 2021–2025
My roleDirector, Marketing & Communications, Stempel College

The challenge

Public health research can be highly specialized, while its implications reach far beyond a university. Stempel College needed communications that connected faculty expertise with audiences including journalists, students and the wider community.

My contribution

I directed marketing and communications across four academic schools, working with faculty and the university’s news team. I wrote research stories, media pitches and press materials; developed a media training program; and prepared speeches, talking points and Q&A for leadership. I also managed staff, the communications budget and cross-functional editorial work.

Two examples

My writing covered research on psychiatric disease risks linked to childhood lead exposure and the practical training behind a large-scale disaster response course. These pieces make the research or learning experience the center of the story, with a clear explanation for a general audience. The linked FIU articles provide the published work.

What this work brings to a team

Experience translating academic expertise, working across institutional teams and helping researchers become effective public voices.
